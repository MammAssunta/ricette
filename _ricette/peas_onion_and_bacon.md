---
name: Peas, onion and bacon
---
# Ingredients

1 onion, chopped or finely sliced

100g unsmoked bacon cubes

200g peas

½ stock cube

Salt to taste 



# Method 

Fry the onion in a little oil until soft.

Add bacon, stir gently.

Add peas and stir carefully and regularly.

Add salt to taste.

Add stock, either by crumbling it in the pot and stirring in in the juices, or by melting it in a cup of boiling water then add the water to the peas.

You can always add more water if you’d like the dish juicier.

 

# Uses: 

1. Eat them by themselves as a side dish.
2. Mix them with pasta for a delicious meal. 

 

**Tip**: if you don’t have bacon cubes, you can either cut your own bacon or you can substitute bacon with cooked ham (always cut in small pieces).
